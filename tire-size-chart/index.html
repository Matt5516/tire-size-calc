Tire size chart

Common sizes by wheel, with height, width and sidewall in inches.

Overall height = wheel + 2 × (width × aspect ÷ 100 ÷ 25.4). For example 265/70R17 is 31.6 in tall, 225/45R17 is 25.0 in and 275/55R20 is 31.9 in. Tables below cover 15 to 22 inch wheels.

Tap any size to open its page. Heights are calculated; real tires vary by brand and model.

FAQ

How tall is a 265/70R17?

31.6 inches.

How do I use a tire size chart?

Find your wheel size, then your size row, to read the height. Compare two rows to see how much taller or shorter a new size is.

Does the chart list popular sizes?

No. It lists typical car, SUV and truck sizes. It is not a ranking or a fit guarantee.
