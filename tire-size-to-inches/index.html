FAQ

How do I convert a tire size to inches?

Height = wheel diameter + 2 × (section width in mm × aspect ratio ÷ 100) ÷ 25.4. Width in inches = section width ÷ 25.4.

How tall is a 265/70R17?

31.6 inches (803 mm).

Is this a list of the most popular sizes?

No. It covers typical car, SUV and truck sizes, not a popularity ranking. Check that a tire is made in your size.
