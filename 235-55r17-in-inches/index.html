235/55R17 in inches: 27.2 x 9.3 R17

Height, width, sidewall and neighbouring sizes for a 235/55R17 tire.

235/55R17 is 27.2 in tall and 9.3 in wide on a 17 in wheel: 27.2x9.3R17. That is 690 mm tall with a 5.1 in sidewall and 742 revolutions per mile. The next wider size up, 245/55R17, is +1.6% in height.

FAQ

How tall is a 235/55R17 tire in inches?

235/55R17 is 27.18 inches (690 mm) tall: 17 + 2 × (235 × 55% ÷ 25.4).

How wide is a 235/55R17?

235/55R17 is 9.25 inches (235 mm) wide at the tread section, with a 5.09 in sidewall.

How many revolutions per mile is a 235/55R17?

About 742.1 revolutions per mile, from a circumference of 85.4 inches.

What does 235/55R17 mean?

235 is the section width in millimetres, 55 is the sidewall height as a percent of the width, R means radial, and 17 is the wheel diameter in inches.

What is the speedometer error going from 235/55R17 to 245/55R17?

245/55R17 is +1.6% in height, so 60 mph on the speedometer would really be 61.0 mph.
