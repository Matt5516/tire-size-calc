145/80R13 in inches: 22.1 x 5.7 R13

Height, width, sidewall and neighbouring sizes for a 145/80R13 tire.

145/80R13 is 22.1 in tall and 5.7 in wide on a 13 in wheel: 22.1x5.7R13. That is 562 mm tall with a 4.6 in sidewall and 911 revolutions per mile. The next wider size up, 155/80R13, is +2.8% in height.

FAQ

How tall is a 145/80R13 tire in inches?

145/80R13 is 22.13 inches (562 mm) tall: 13 + 2 × (145 × 80% ÷ 25.4).

How wide is a 145/80R13?

145/80R13 is 5.71 inches (145 mm) wide at the tread section, with a 4.57 in sidewall.

How many revolutions per mile is a 145/80R13?

About 911.2 revolutions per mile, from a circumference of 69.5 inches.

What does 145/80R13 mean?

145 is the section width in millimetres, 80 is the sidewall height as a percent of the width, R means radial, and 13 is the wheel diameter in inches.

What is the speedometer error going from 145/80R13 to 155/80R13?

155/80R13 is +2.8% in height, so 60 mph on the speedometer would really be 61.7 mph.
